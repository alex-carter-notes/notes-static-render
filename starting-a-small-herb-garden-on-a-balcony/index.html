Starting a Small Herb Garden on a Balcony

Alex Carter · 2026-11-25

Growing herbs in pots is one of the easiest ways to start gardening. A sunny balcony is enough for basil, parsley and chives, and the whole setup costs very little.

Choose a pot with drainage holes and fill it with light potting mix. If you want a longer checklist of containers and soil, see this balcony herb guide for the basics.

Light and water

Most herbs need around six hours of sun. Water when the top two centimetres of soil feel dry, and avoid leaving water in the saucer, because roots rot quickly in standing water.

Harvesting

Pick leaves often from the top so the plant grows bushier. Never remove more than a third of the plant at once.
